Theorem library

Tactic reference

Implication and ∀introexactapply

intro h assumes the premise of an implication, or fixes an arbitrary element for a forall. exact e closes the goal with a hypothesis or theorem. apply e reduces the goal to the premises of e.

Conjunction and ↔split.left.right

split proves both halves. Use h.left and h.right to take a conjunction or biconditional apart; obtain hp hq := h names both halves.

Disjunctionleftrightcases

left or right picks a side of the goal. cases h with followed by | left hp => and | right hq => arms analyzes a hypothesis.

Existentialsexistsobtain

exists t supplies a witness. obtain x hx := h or cases h with | intro x hx => unpacks one.

Evaluationdecide

decide closes a goal with no unknowns by evaluating it: arithmetic on numerals, data constructors, defined predicates, and quantifiers over a data type whose values are a fixed list of constants. If the goal is false, it says which part.

Equality and definitionsreflrewriteunfoldsimp

refl closes a = b when both sides compute to the same term. rewrite -> h replaces the left side of the equation h by its right side (<- for the other direction, at h2 to rewrite a hypothesis). unfold Name and simp expand definitions.

Negation and absurditycontradictionexfalso

not P is P -> False. contradiction closes a goal from False or from P and not P. exfalso turns any goal into False.

Classical (needs mode classical)by_casesby_contra

by_cases h : P splits on whether P holds. by_contra h assumes the goal is false and asks for a contradiction.

Structurehaveshowsorry

have h : P opens a subgoal for P; have h : P := e or := by with an indented block proves it in place. show P restates the goal. sorry leaves a goal unproved and marks the theorem incomplete.
